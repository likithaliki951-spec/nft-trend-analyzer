---
name: NFT data provenance
description: The user's data-source constraint for NFT market and social-media analysis.
---

Do not invent or present fabricated OpenSea or social-media information as live or real data. Analyze user-supplied datasets, and clearly label any synthetic examples.

**Why:** The student project must distinguish illustrative sample values from actual market evidence.

**How to apply:** Do not imply external data connections exist; disclose missing fields and label synthetic sources wherever they appear.
